Our Mission

Every year, millions of tons of food go to waste while many go hungry. SmartFood bridges this gap by creating a seamless platform where food surplus becomes surplus hope. We empower restaurants, hotels, and food businesses to donate responsibly while helping NGOs access fresh food efficiently.
